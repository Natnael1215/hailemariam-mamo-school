Admission Information

Welcome to Hailemariam Mamo Secondary High School. Students who are interested in joining our school can follow the official admission procedures.

Admission Requirements

  • Student identification document
  • Previous school certificate or report
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Completed admission form

Grades

Admission information is available for eligible students according to the school admission policy.

Admission Process

  1. Visit the school administration office.
  2. Ask for the current admission requirements.
  3. Complete the registration form.
  4. Submit the required documents.
  5. Follow the instructions provided by the school.

For more information, please contact the school administration.
